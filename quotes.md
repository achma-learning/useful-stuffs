# Quotes

## The three powers

Three powers: wisdom / الحكمة, money / المال, connection / العلاقات.

Humor is also a power, depending on the situation. Knowledge can sometimes be good, or just noise. Money can unlock things, or become a burden. Connection can unlock doors, or turn into a toxic relationship.

## Network

Your network is your net worth.

المرء على دين خليله فلينظر أحدكم من يخالل
